# Conceptual Architecture

## Purpose

SECAV-O is an initial ontology-supported model for connecting software-engineering work with developer competency, observable evidence, human validation, and coaching.

It does not attempt to replace software-development methodologies, maturity models, architecture methods, security frameworks, or existing software-engineering ontologies.

## Core pattern

```text
Engineer
  │ performs
  ▼
Engineering Activity
  ├── requiresCompetency ──> Competency
  ├── produces ────────────> Artifact
  ├── assessedAgainst ─────> Quality / Acceptance Criterion
  ├── measuredBy ──────────> Measurement
  ├── assistedByAI ────────> AI System
  └── requiresHumanValidation ──> Human Validation Activity

Evidence
  ├── providesEvidenceOf ──> Competency
  └── triggers ────────────> Coaching Intervention

Competency Assessment
  ├── basedOnEvidence ─────> Evidence
  └── assessesCompetency ──> Competency

Coaching Intervention
  └── targetsCompetency ───> Competency
```

## Separation of implementation and review

Creation and evaluation are modeled as different activities because they can require different competencies and produce different evidence.

Example:

```text
Application Design
├── Design Implementation
└── Design Review

Unit Test Design
├── Unit Test Design Implementation
└── Unit Test Design Review
```

## Competency orientation

The framework does not infer competency merely from years of experience, job title, or completion of a course.

Evidence may include:
- work products;
- review findings;
- defect records;
- test results;
- measurements;
- repeated observations of engineering activity.

## AI assistance and human validation

AI assistance is modeled explicitly rather than hidden inside the activity.

The prototype distinguishes:
- the engineering activity;
- the AI system that assisted it;
- the artifact produced or modified;
- the human-validation activity;
- acceptance criteria;
- evidence generated by review.

## Ontology reuse strategy

The project intends to align with existing software-engineering ontologies where appropriate.

SEON is being evaluated as a candidate reference for process, activity, artifact, design, coding, testing, quality, and measurement concepts:

https://dev.nemo.inf.ufes.br/seon/SEON.html

Version 0.1 deliberately uses local SECAV-O concepts and `rdfs:seeAlso` rather than `owl:imports`. Direct imports or equivalence mappings should be added only after identifiers, semantics, maintenance status, and reuse/licensing conditions are verified.

## Validation layer

OWL/RDFS expresses vocabulary and semantic relationships.

SHACL expresses selected graph constraints. These are prototype governance/data-quality rules, not empirical claims about organizational effectiveness.

## Version 0.1 boundary

v0.1 is intentionally small. It provides enough structure to load the ontology in an RDF/OWL tool, create sample instances, validate selected graph constraints, review the competency model, conduct expert review, and design a future pilot.

It does not establish that the framework is complete, standardized, validated, or adopted.
